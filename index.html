Background

I recently defended my PhD in Food Science at the University of Arkansas, Fayetteville (degree conferral expected Dec 2026), working under Dr. Dongyi Wang in the SAFE Lab, where I also serve as a Graduate Assistant and Teaching Assistant in Biological and Agricultural Engineering. My research sits at the intersection of intelligent sensing, multimodal imaging, machine learning, and robotics, applied to real-world food quality and safety challenges.

Before joining UARK, I completed my B.Tech. in Food Technology at Dr. NTR College of Food Science and Technology in India. That foundation in food science principles now drives my engineering and AI work.

My PhD grew from classical ML into full multimodal systems. I integrate hyperspectral cameras, thermal sensors, depth point clouds, transillumination imaging, and tactile robotics into tools like NAS-WD, MyoVision, and TACTUS that detect chicken breast myopathies and contaminants non-invasively and in real time. Along the way I have mentored nine students, filed a patent, and co-authored 14 published papers, with 5 more under review.
